# ADR-0001: Arm size, payload and servo per joint

- **Status:** accepted by the owner, 2026-11-15 (Option B)
- **Spec:** §4.2, §4.3, D2, D16
- **Numbers:** [torque_budget.md](../design/torque_budget.md), generated by `tools/torque_budget.py`

## Decision

- **Links:** upper arm 80 mm, forearm 80 mm, wrist axis to grip centre 95 mm.
- **Reach:** 255 mm to the grip centre (270 mm to the fingertips).
- **Rated payload:** **50 g** at full horizontal reach.

| Joint | Servo | SF at 50 g (Phase 0 estimates) | Why this servo |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1 base yaw | MG996R | 14.3 | Load is small, but metal gears survive knocks on the long arm, and one model for J1–J3 means one spare covers three joints. |
| J2 shoulder | MG996R | **2.33** | Strongest allowed servo. This joint limits the whole design. |
| J3 elbow | MG996R | 4.9 | An S3003 would be lighter but falls below SF 2. |
| J4 wrist pitch | MG90S | 2.73 | An S3003 is stronger, but its extra 24 g at the wrist costs the shoulder more than it gains. |
| J5 wrist roll | MG90S | 6.7 | |
| J6 gripper | MG90S | n/a | Metal gears; SG90 gears strip when squeezing. |

## Why 50 g and not 75 g

The same arm passes at 75 g, but only with a 4 % margin on hand-estimated part masses. Rating it at 50 g leaves about 16 % reserve at the shoulder for parts that turn out heavier than estimated. The owner chose the honest rating over the optimistic one.

## Sizing gate (owner requirement)

`tools/export_all.py` reruns the torque budget on the **CAD-derived** masses every time the CAD is exported (`torque_budget.py --gate`). If any joint falls below SF 2.0 at 50 g, the export fails and work stops until the owner has been told.

## Caveats

- **SF 2 against the datasheet is roughly SF 1.4 against a weak clone** (60 % of rated torque). Buy from a seller with good reviews, and buy spares.
- **More than about 75 g needs one of the §4.3 options** (dual shoulder servos, counterbalance spring, parallelogram linkage). Those are major changes and are the owner's call.

## Method notes

- Bought-part masses come from datasheets. Printed parts were estimated in Phase 0 and are replaced by CAD volumes × PETG density × fill factor in Phase 1.
- Pots are always assumed fitted on the joints that can have them (J1–J4), because that is the heavier case.
- Servo data cross-checked online (MG996R: 9.4 / 11 kgf·cm, 2.5 A stall, 55 g, 40.7 × 19.7 × 42.9 mm; MG90S: 13.4 g, 1.8 / 2.2 kgf·cm). Sources disagree on the MG90S spline (20T or 21T); this does not matter because parts are screwed to the servo's own horn.
